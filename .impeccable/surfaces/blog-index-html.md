# Blog do Lumens

Scope: blog/index.html and every blog/<slug>/index.html (generated by scripts/blog/gerar.mjs). Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: a couple planning a wedding in BH reads an article signed by Delliz Christine, gets oriented with sources, and may check the date on WhatsApp. Constraints: no invented facts; Delliz approves every text before it is published.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lumens means light. The reading happens in nude daylight and the end of each article falls into night. It refuses the generic blog of identical grey cards and stock photos.

OWN-WORLD: nude day (--dia, --dia-2), green for actions and details (--verde), gold for brilliance (--ouro text, --ouro-linha rules, --luz on night), orange "lume" only in points (focus, the light marking the TOC, cover lights, the sunset band --anoitecer, which the user explicitly asked for as gold-and-orange light). Night green --noite. Bodoni Moda + Manrope. The brand arch frames every cover.

STORY: the reader sees who writes (byline with photo), reads "Em resumo", navigates through the TOC, uses the highlight boxes (checklist, deadlines, warning, church norm), checks the sources and closes on the night with Delliz and the WhatsApp button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: article on desktop with the title, standfirst and byline on the left and the arch cover (gold line art on night green, a chip with the topic) on the right; on mobile, an arch-topped band above the breadcrumb and the title. Index: an h1 with Delliz's byline, then the featured article in a portrait arch plus two cards in banded arches.

FORM: pinned by the user (palette, the four structure improvements, the "another kind" of texture); an extension of the incumbent world, with no concept roll owed (new-work.md, extend an existing surface). Signature: covers drawn in scripts/blog/capas.mjs (rings, string of lights, music staff), identical on the page and in the share image; a sticky TOC with a light that turns on at the section being read.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
