## Direction contract

THESIS: the lit door. The brand's double church arch opens the page: on the first visit the Lumes Experience logo draws itself on the brand peach and its inner arch becomes a door that opens onto the site. Then the wedding day turns into night: the ceremony in light peach, the reception in a deep terracotta-brown night lit by peach. It refuses the wedding-band page with a full-screen photo and a list of services.

OWN-WORLD: the owner's visual identity, delivered 2026-11-03 and pinned (terracotta #bb7944, peach #edc79b, the double arch, the high-contrast L with a small star and a four-point star with eight light rays, LUMES / EXPERIENCE in geometric caps, vectorized in scripts/blog/marca.mjs). Brand terracotta only on the logo and ornaments (it fails text contrast); deep terracotta #8E5226 for CTAs, links and numerals; night --noite #2B1A0F with peach --luz buttons; the sunset band --anoitecer from peach through terracotta to night, with the logo star lit in it. "Luz" texture: soft peach/terracotta halos, no noise. Bodoni Moda + Manrope; church arch. Body text justified with pt-BR hyphenation (owner's request).

STORY: (first visit) the intro and the door; hear Delliz, see the music moment by moment from the ceremony to the dance floor, understand the formation and the sound, check the date. The "Ver a abertura com música" button replays the intro with a synced Web Audio score.
